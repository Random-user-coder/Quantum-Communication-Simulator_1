What the model computes

Two independent stages reduce the number of usable photons. The fibre attenuates exponentially with distance; the detector registers only a fraction of what arrives.

T = 10−αL / 10    Pdet = T · η    Ndet = Nsent · Pdet

Every number in this interface follows from those three lines. Adjust the controls to explore the link.

01 / Fibre

Transmission through fibre

T = 10−αL / 10

α is attenuation in dB/km and L is distance in km. Each 10 dB of loss leaves one tenth of the photons.

With the current settings: T = 10^(−0.20 × 100 / 10) = 0.01.

02 / Detector

Probability of registration

Pdet = T × η

The detector efficiency η is the probability that an arriving photon produces a registered event.

Pdet = 0.01 × 0.80 = 0.008.

03 / Counts

Expected detections

E[Ndet] = Nsent × Pdet

This is an expected count, not a guarantee. For independent photons, count fluctuations are approximately binomial and often close to Poisson at small probabilities.

1,000,000,000 × 0.008 = 8,000,000 expected detections.

Assumptions / limits

What this model leaves out

Uniform single-mode fibre; constant attenuation; independent photons; one detector with fixed efficiency.

It does not model dark counts, detector dead time, timing jitter, coupling loss, connectors, polarisation drift, background light, or a QKD protocol. Real links can perform worse.
